One* Night at Miku's (2026)

GitHub | Download

Platforms: Windows | Linux
Project Length: 6 months
Engine and Tools: C# | Monogame

About

One* Night at Miku's is a rogue-like action spin on classic Five Nights at Freddy's sit-and-survive gameplay. Study, learn, and master the machinations of the various nightly threats that stand between you and 6 AM.

Features

  • Fast-paced and addictive management gameplay where the most important resource is information, and the most costly action is hesitation.
  • 10+ gameplay modifiers (rogue-like style upgrades).
  • Compelling enemy AI.
  • Interesting(?) story.
  • 3 difficulty modes.
  • Lots and lots of easter eggs.
  • All the extras you'd expect of a FNaF style game (Custom night, etc.)
